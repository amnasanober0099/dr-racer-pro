Tic Tac Toe

Player X's turn
How to Play
  • Choose Vs Computer or 2 Players mode.
  • Players take turns marking X and O.
  • First to get 3 in a row (line, column, diagonal) wins.
  • Winning line will be highlighted.
  • Enjoy, and see Good/Amazing effects when you win!
