How to Play

  • Shoot bubbles to form groups of 3 or more of the same color to pop them!
  • Disconnected bubbles will drop and vanish (not fall to the bottom).
  • Use mouse or touch to aim, then click/tap to shoot.
  • Game Over: if any bubble reaches the bottom line.
