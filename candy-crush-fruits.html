How to Play
  • Swap two adjacent fruits to match three or more of the same fruit in a row or column.
  • Matched fruits will disappear, and new fruits will fall from above to fill the gaps.
  • Earn points with every match! Try to get the highest score before your moves run out.
  • Enter your name in the leaderboard to set a record.
  • Special combos: Match 4 or more for bonus points!
