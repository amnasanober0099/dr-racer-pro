How to Play
  • Click a tube to select, then click another tube to pour the top color.
  • Only same color can be poured (or empty tube), tube must not be full.
  • Each tube has 4 layers. Complete when all tubes are sorted by color.
  • Try to solve in minimum moves—add your name to the leaderboard!
