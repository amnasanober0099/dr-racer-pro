Controls: / Move Lane Accelerate Brake Swipe on game for mobile controls!

How to Play

  • Desktop: Use Left/Right Arrow keys to change lanes, Up Arrow to accelerate, and Down Arrow to brake.
  • Mobile: Swipe left/right to change lanes, Swipe up to accelerate, Swipe down to brake.
  • Avoid all other cars. If you crash, it's game over!
  • Your score increases the longer you survive. Speed increases as you go. Good luck!
